01

Times Internet

Marketing · Events & Community

Events Community Paid Media

Marketing as an Ecosystem

Built a multi-stakeholder go-to-market for TSAC & NBT Mic Drop Madness: paid, partnerships, and people.

The Challenge

Events are perhaps one of the hardest products to market. People don't buy an event for what it is — they buy into what they believe it could become for them. A student looked for opportunities, a parent looked for security, a university looked for visibility.

Contribution

Meta ad campaigns, competitor analysis, market research, audience segmentation, influencer marketing, and barter partnerships. Reached out to 100+ stakeholders across institutions, partners, and collaborators.

What I Took Away

Great marketing begins with understanding people. Data helps you understand what people do, but empathy helps you understand why. You're never really marketing an event — you're creating opportunities for people, ideas, and experiences to come together.

400+ Registrations
250+ Community in 20 days
100+ Stakeholders
02

The Indian Express

B2B Events · Lead Strategy

B2B CXO Outreach Lead Gen

Data, Then Conversation

Five Oracle / IBM / SAS-backed CXO summits in two months. Built the pipeline from scratch.

The Challenge

High-profile B2B events for industry leaders across 5 summits in just two months. Thousands of potential prospects existed but there was no structured pipeline to identify who mattered, how to reach them, or how to make them say yes.

Contribution

Using Apollo and EasyLeadz, I mapped and filtered 1,000+ prospects. Personally reached out to 50+ CXOs, conducted 100+ outreach calls, and participated in on-ground execution. Every interaction taught me that B2B marketing is as much about listening as pitching.

What I Took Away

Data and people are two completely different problems — you have to be good at both. The tools get you the name. The conversation gets them to show up. When you're on a call with a CXO, you have 30 seconds before they've decided whether you're worth their time.

+30% Qualified leads
1,000+ Prospects mapped
100+ CXOs in the room
03

Care Trust

Social Impact · CSR Research

CSR Field Ops Research

Empathy Meets Systems Thinking

Menstrual hygiene awareness + sustainable product strategy across schools and villages.

The Challenge

Menstrual hygiene is often seen as a health issue. Working at Care Trust revealed the challenge is much larger: lack of awareness, limited access, and social stigma — compounded by environmental impact from non-biodegradable products.

Contribution

Collaborated with 5+ government schools, organised 5+ awareness events, coordinated village outreach, helped distribute 200+ sanitary pads, and researched CSR opportunities across Noida. Also conducted competitor analysis for menstrual cup scaling.

What I Took Away

Many important challenges persist not because solutions don't exist, but because awareness and access don't reach everyone equally. Lasting impact happens when good intentions are backed by data, execution, and sustainable solutions.

500+ People impacted
200+ Pads distributed
5+ Schools partnered
04

Miskaveda

Brand · Social Media

D2C Social Media Content

Built From Zero

Built Miskaveda's digital presence from zero across Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn.

The Challenge

When I joined Miskaveda, the brand existed, but its digital presence didn't — no Instagram, no Facebook, no LinkedIn. In a beauty industry dominated by Forest Essentials and Kama Ayurveda, standing out as a new brand with limited resources required both creativity and clarity.

Contribution

Built presence across three platforms, bridged founder and creative team, segmented audiences (18–45), created content around three pillars: educational, relatable, and trending. Collaborated with 3 influencers. Weekly analytics informed strategy refinement.

What I Took Away

Building something from nothing teaches you things that maintaining something never can. When resources are limited, clarity becomes your biggest advantage.

0→400 Organic followers
3 Platforms built
3 Influencer collabs

Content That Scales

Led social media strategy and a 10-member content team across two brands.
